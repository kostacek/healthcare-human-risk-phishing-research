# Executive Security Briefing: Healthcare GRC Vulnerability Assessment

## 1. Scope of Evaluation
This report summarizes the operational risk footprint of the core electronic health record (EHR) pipelines and human-factor vulnerabilities across the clinical environment.

## 2. Critical Findings & Control Gaps
* **Elevated Social Vulnerability:** Current training strategies rely on passive compliance videos. Threat modeling shows high susceptibility to authority-based social engineering cues.
* **Lack of Formal Reporting Paths:** While staff show basic technical alertness to suspicious indicators, there is no centralized, single-click ticketing workflow to flag active exploits to IT security teams.

## 3. Immediate Action Plan
1. Deploy contextual phishing simulation controls mimicking vendor-level communications.
2. Update administrative safety frameworks to comply directly with HIPAA § 164.308.

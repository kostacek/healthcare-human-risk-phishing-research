# Corporate Information Security Policy: Access Control & IAM

## 1. Purpose
This policy defines requirements for identity management and logical access boundaries to ensure complete compliance with the HIPAA Security Rule and the NIST Cyber Security Framework.

## 2. Enforcement & Technical Controls
* **Role-Based Access Control (RBAC):** Access to patient databases is strictly limited by clinical operational roles (e.g., Dentist, Hygienist, Assistant, Administrator).
* **Authentication Safeguards:** Multi-factor authentication (MFA) is mandatory for all active directories. Sessions must automatically lock out after 10 minutes of clinical inactivity to prevent endpoint tampering.
